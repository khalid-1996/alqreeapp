Privacy Policy

Alqaree · Last updated: October 12, 2026

Who we are

Alqaree ("القارئ") is a free app for listening to the Holy Quran, reading adhkar and a short daily reminder from the Quran and Sunnah, made by an individual developer. This policy covers the Android and iOS app, the Alqaree TikTok account, and the tool that posts to it.

What we collect

  • No account or sign-in.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number or location, and we use no tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • Favorites, adhkar progress, last played and settings stay on your device and are removed with the app.

Network

The app calls public services to stream recitations and radio and to update the daily content. These requests carry nothing that identifies you, though those services can see your IP address like any website.

Notifications

Notifications are scheduled locally on your device; we do not send push messages from a server. You can turn them off or reduce them in Settings.

TikTok

We use the TikTok API only to post to our own Alqaree account: one video a day with that day's reminder. Our server stores our own account's access token and a posting log. We do not access or collect data of any other TikTok user.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
